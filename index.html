Where your letters live

The whole storage model, in three sentences.

On this device

Every letter is written to this browser’s local storage under a single key. There is no account, no database, and no sync between devices.

Nothing is sent

The page loads a stylesheet, a script, and its fonts. Nothing you type is transmitted anywhere — the countdown runs entirely on your own clock.

Yours to keep or lose

Clearing site data, browsing privately, or switching browsers removes your letters permanently. Treat them the way you would a paper journal.
